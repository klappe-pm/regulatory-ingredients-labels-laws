# reflection

Prompts that turn Copilot back on your own use of it: reviewing how you prompt, spotting recurring tasks worth turning into reusable saved prompts or skills, and running a short periodic retro on what is and is not working. This is the category that keeps the rest of the library improving, since a beginner LLM user gets better fastest by looking at their own transcripts with help.

Run [[prompting-style-review]] after a week or two of real use, [[skill-gap-finder]] whenever the same manual task keeps recurring, and [[copilot-usage-retro]] on a monthly cadence.

## prompts

- [[prompting-style-review]]: paste recent Copilot chats; get concrete, prioritized feedback on how to prompt better.
- [[skill-gap-finder]]: from your recurring tasks, identify which deserve a saved prompt, a template, or a full Copilot skill, and draft the top candidate.
- [[copilot-usage-retro]]: a monthly retro on what worked, what wasted time, and what to change next month.

## workflow

1. Use the library normally for a couple of weeks.
2. Run [[prompting-style-review]] on a sample of your real chats to sharpen technique.
3. Run [[skill-gap-finder]] to convert your most repetitive tasks into reusable assets, feeding results to [[durable-knowledge]] and [[copilot-configuration]] for install.
4. Run [[copilot-usage-retro]] monthly and let its output steer next month's focus.

# content-derivation

Prompts that derive the regulated content of a label directly from the formula and specification: the ingredient statement, the allergen declaration, and the Nutrition Facts Panel. These are drafting prompts, not review prompts; each output still needs a downstream verification pass ([[review-and-verification]]) before it is treated as final.

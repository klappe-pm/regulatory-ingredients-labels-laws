# regwatch — what this is, in plain language

_For the Talking Rain team. Prepared for review by Diane Tran (dtran@talkingrain.com)._

## What This Is

A small, internal tool that watches food regulation and turns it into a short,
readable briefing for our team. It focuses on FDA food-safety and labeling
changes that directly affect beverages in the United States, plus what we can
glean from Canada and Mexico, the other markets we sell into. Every item is
written the same way: a plain summary, the impact, what it means for the
beverage business, and a link to the original source so you can verify it.

## Why We Built It

Federal rules and a fast-moving patchwork of state laws now land on the same can
at the same time. The goal is not to predict which rule wins; it is to stay
organized enough to move quickly when any of them lands. This tool keeps one
clean, sourced list of what is changing so we are never surprised by a
deadline — or by a lawsuit.

## Outcomes We Want to Drive at Talking Rain

- One shared, trustworthy view of what is changing, instead of scattered emails.
- Earlier warning on reformulation deadlines (for example dyes, BVO, Red No. 3,
  and California's 2027 dates) so R&D and suppliers have lead time.
- Claims and label changes reviewed before artwork, not after a complaint.
- A simple record we can hand to leadership, brand, and legal that everyone
  reads the same way.

## How You Will Get It

The briefing comes in three depths so it fits the reader:

- **v1 (headlines)** — a one-screen read for everyone.
- **v2 (context and the rule)** — adds the why and the legal citation, for leads.
- **v3 (full detail)** — adds the action, the affected products, and a to-do
  list, for whoever owns the response.

## How Often

To be decided — and we want your input. Options on the table are a monthly
digest, a quarterly deep dive, or an as-needed alert when something material
changes. Tell us what cadence would actually be useful to your team.

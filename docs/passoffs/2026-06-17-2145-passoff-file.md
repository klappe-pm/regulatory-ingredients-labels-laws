focused on FDA food safety impacting US beverages (Canada and Mexico as the
other markets), written as What Happened / What It Means / What We Need To Do,

## Key Decisions

- Scope refocused to FDA food safety impacting US beverages, with a US / Canada
  / Mexico country breakdown ("Alaska" read as a transcription slip for the USA,
  confirmed with the owner).
- Recent developments are driven by a dated change log, not only the source-hash
  scan, so the briefing reflects more than one change. Events are real,
  verifiable milestones; the analyst maintains currency.
- Warning letters are modeled as recurring enforcement themes with governing CFR
  and a link to the live FDA database, not fabricated specific letters.
- CFR citations use eCFR links and are part-level where a precise subsection is
  not certain; everything is flagged for confirmation against primary text.

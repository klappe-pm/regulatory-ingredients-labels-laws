# gensess-law-talk

A conversational "Law Talk Live" survey of the coming regulatory year across FDA and FSIS, framed by two practitioners.

## Summary

Chris Forgues (Assistant General Counsel for Food, Feed, and Products Law at Cargill) and Tony Pavel (Partner at Keller and Heckman) presented a live, lightly structured discussion of the year ahead. The deck is image-driven with section titles only, so the substance was carried verbally rather than on the slides; this note captures the agenda at title level and should be supplemented by Diane's notes or recording if available.

## Key Points

The session moved through FDA and FSIS leadership changes, a MAHA update playfully titled around tallow, the cluster of GRAS, UPF, and front-of-pack developments, FDA post-market review of BHA and BHT alongside a note on rosemary extract status in Canada, state and federal legislative activity with a caution that individual bills may be counted under more than one policy area, FSIS relocation and other developments, and the "Product of USA" labeling question.

## Takeaways

The agenda mirrors the heavier policy decks in this set, particularly the MAHA, state-initiatives, and regulatory-landscape sessions, but adds a USDA-FSIS lens through leadership changes, FSIS relocation, and Product of USA labeling. Because the deck itself is title-level, treat the cross-referenced sessions as the substantive source and use this note as a pointer.

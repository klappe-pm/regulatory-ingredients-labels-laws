## Beverage Lens

Category-specific reading of where the portfolio meets the rules.

### Sweeteners and added sugar

Added sugar is the most likely front-of-package High rating and the clearest SNAP and school target, while non-nutritive sweeteners such as sucralose draw ultra-processed-food definitions and labeling challenges. The win and the risk sit on the same zero-sugar can.

- Affected products: Sparkling Ice (classic flavors), Sparkling Ice +Caffeine, Sparkling Ice Starburst
- Related obligations: California Non-Ultraprocessed Certified Seal, California School Ultra-Processed Food Law, Mexico NOM-051 Front-Of-Pack Warning Labels, SNAP Sweetened Beverage Restrictions, State Ingredient Restriction Patchwork, Ultra-Processed Food Definition
- Action: Confirm sweetener disclosures and watch front-of-package, UPF, SNAP, California AB 1264, and Mexico NOM-051.

### Synthetic colors

Flavored and colored sparkling drinks are squarely in the federal dye phase-out and state school bans, and any color swap is also a claims event, not just a recipe change.

- Affected products: Sparkling Ice (classic flavors), Sparkling Ice +Caffeine, Sparkling Ice Starburst
- Related obligations: California Food Safety Act Ingredient Ban, California School Food Dye Ban, Iowa School Food Ingredient Law, Nebraska School Food Ingredient Law, New Jersey Ingredient Restriction Bill, State Ingredient Restriction Patchwork, Synthetic Dye Phase-Out, Tennessee School Food Ingredient Law, West Virginia Ingredient Ban
- Action: Inventory color sources by SKU and route any swap through claims review.

### Caffeine and energy positioning

Caffeinated lines carry their own track: voluntary federal caffeine labeling guidance and Canada's supplemented-foods caution statements and caffeine limits.

- Affected products: Sparkling Ice +Caffeine
- Related obligations: Caffeine Labeling Guidance, Canada Supplemented Foods Caution Statements
- Action: Confirm per-serving caffeine and cautionary-statement requirements per market.

### Preservatives and the GRAS overhaul

Self-affirmed GRAS additives now need an audit-ready safety file, and preservatives such as BHA and BHT are early federal re-review targets.

- Related obligations: California Food Safety Act Ingredient Ban, California State GRAS Reporting Bill, GRAS Self-Affirmation Overhaul, New Jersey Ingredient Restriction Bill, New Jersey State GRAS Reporting Bill, New York State GRAS Reporting Bill, State Ingredient Restriction Patchwork, West Virginia Ingredient Ban
- Action: Assemble safety files for any self-affirmed GRAS ingredient in use.

### Proposition 65 heavy metals

Trace heavy metals carried in by botanicals, colors, or concentrates create bounty-driven Proposition 65 exposure where presence, not harm, triggers a notice.

- Related obligations: Proposition 65 Warning Requirement
- Action: Maintain annual heavy-metal testing and supplier indemnity for California-sold products.
